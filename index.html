What Is Neonpath?

Neonpath is a minimalist puzzle game that combines sleek neon visuals with intuitive gameplay. Your goal is to connect dots of the same color, creating paths that don’t cross. As levels progress, the puzzles become more intricate, pushing your planning and spatial reasoning skills to the limit.

Designed for players who appreciate clean design and thoughtful challenges, Neonpath turns simple dot connections into an artful experience.

How to Play Neonpath

Starting Neonpath is straightforward. Your screen will display a grid dotted with pairs of neon-colored points. Your task is to draw paths connecting matching dots without overlapping the lines. Completing all connections unlocks the next level.
As you advance, the puzzles introduce new grid sizes and more complex layouts, encouraging you to think ahead and plan your moves carefully.
It’s a relaxing yet stimulating way to challenge your mind anytime, anywhere.
